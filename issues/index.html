Report a problem

Before writing, open PairDesk on the computer with the problem and press ? > Check this computer. It often says exactly what to fix. In a terminal the same check is pairdesk doctor.

Send us the details

Use the contact form on knowreva.com and include:

  • What you did and what happened instead.
  • The report from Check this computer (its Copy report button puts it on the clipboard).
  • Your Linux version, for example Ubuntu 26.04 or Debian 13.
  • The last lines of the log: ? > Open log file.

The report and log contain computer names and addresses on your network, but no passwords or keys.
